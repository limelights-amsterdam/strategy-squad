================================================================
WHY — strategy-squad (the design rationale)
================================================================

WHAT
A Claude Code plugin that runs McKinsey, BCG and Bain as three rival AI
associates on one strategy case, lets them fight, builds on the wreckage with a
Blue Team, and then prepares the human facilitator who walks into the room.

WHY THREE FIRMS INSTEAD OF ONE
One model giving "an opinion" is easy to nod along to. Three house styles that
genuinely disagree force the real choice into the open. The value isn't any single
firm's answer — it's the TENSION between them:
  - McKinsey structures the problem (and risks a tidy consensus answer).
  - BCG runs the economics (and risks being cold and people-blind).
  - Bain ranks the work and asks who owns it (and risks hugging the safe core).
Each firm's blind spot is written into its cheatsheet, and the other firms attack
it. A weakness that ≥2 independent judges call fatal doesn't survive. That's how
you get an answer that survives a boardroom, not just a slide.

WHY A BLUE TEAM AFTER THE ROAST
The Big 3 are a red team — they shoot holes. Holes are where uncontested market
space hides. The Blue Team turns the most brutal critiques into Blue Ocean moves
("only is better than best") instead of leaving the room demoralized.

WHY TRAIN-THE-TRAINER
A strategist-facilitator's real job at a leadership off-site isn't to have the
answer — it's to ask the questions nobody dares to. So the squad's last move is to
convert the roast into the sharp, provocative questions the facilitator walks in
with, plus a 2-minute pitch. The analysis is input; the questions are the
deliverable.

WHY LOOP MODE IS HUMAN-GATED
Engineering can run autonomously for hours because tests and CI tell it when it's
done. Strategy has no such oracle — no test says "this strategy is correct." So
loop mode DEEPENS until the squad stops surfacing new fatal weaknesses, then STOPS
at a human-review gate. It never finalizes a strategy unattended. This is the whole
point: AI does the ranking and the heatmaps; the senior human owns the decision and
the room. The recipe is free. The restaurant is what matters.

WHY IT COMPOUNDS
Each run banks reusable insights (per client, per market, per recurring pattern) in
docs/strategy-insights/, and the next run reads them first. The squad gets sharper
over time instead of starting cold every session.

LINEAGE
Framework material informed by Oria AI (McKinsey & BCG packs) and Bain's public
methodology. Plugin packaging, the autonomous-loop idea, and the compounding store
are modeled on EveryInc's compound-engineering-plugin (Kieran Klaassen). The
orchestration, cheatsheets, championship, Blue Team and facilitator layer are
original. A sequel to limelights-amsterdam/mckinsey-strategy-team.
